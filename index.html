⚜ About Me ⚜

I'm a full-stack developer with nine years of experience building web apps, from the screens people use every day to the servers and databases behind them. I work mostly in JavaScript, TypeScript, Vue, Python, and PostgreSQL, and I care a lot about making software that's accessible and easy to use.

❀✿❀✿❀

Before development, I worked in psychology, healthcare, and education, including time as a hospital corpsman in the U.S. Navy. That background shapes how I build, because I'm always thinking about the real person on the other end and whether they can actually get their job done.

❀✿❀✿❀

Right now I'm one of the main developers behind a farming app that growers use in the field to check soil and water conditions and to run their irrigation. I'm open to remote roles where I can keep building thoughtful, useful software with a great team.
